# Filter on vs B-match, and lost/gained completions (saved final-test data only)

Generated by `scripts/paper_bmatch_transitions.py` and checked by `scripts/verify_bmatch_transitions.py`
(record: `bmatch_transitions_verification.md`). There was no simulation, no new layouts, no new test and no
verdict. The existing `README.md` in this directory still covers the earlier revision outputs.

## `on_vs_bmatch.csv`: pre-declared secondary, descriptive

This is locked protocol secondary item 6: "Filter-on versus B-match at α\*: on the same pairs and the same
endpoints ((a), (b) and (c) definitions with B-match in place of filter off, and the secondaries above),
with CIs. Nothing confirmatory follows from it."

- **Pairs:** all 9,000 pairs at each density K = 1, 2, 3, with nine actors × 1,000 layouts.
- **Rows:** `pooled` (the density), `seed`, and `actor`.
- **Binary endpoints** (`GOAL`; `FAIL` = COLLISION or INFEASIBLE; `TIMEOUT`):
  - 2×2 counts: `a` = event in both arms, `b` = filter on only, `c` = B-match only, `d` = neither.
  - `estimate` = (b − c)/n, always filter on minus B-match.
  - `m10_lo`/`m10_hi`: two-sided 95% Newcombe (1998) **method 10**. It uses Wilson score limits for each
    arm, with the correlation φ continuity-corrected: the numerator ad − bc becomes
    max(ad − bc − n/2, 0) when ad − bc > 0. The limits are clipped to [−1, 1].
  - `m8_lo`/`m8_hi`: method 8 (uncorrected φ). This matches what `cbf_study/stats.py` implements under
    the name method 10. It is reported for comparison only. Neither `cbf_study/stats.py` nor any
    historical interval was changed.
  - **Undefined correlation:** if any marginal total (a+b, c+d, a+c, b+d) is zero, a marginal variance
    is zero and φ is undefined, so φ = 0 is used. This applies to `FAIL` everywhere, because B-match had
    no COLLISION and no INFEASIBLE outcome.
  - The interval is always computed. Zero discordance does not give [0, 0].
- **`FORMATION_M`:**
  - Per pair, d_i = mean over t = 1..L_i of [e_on(t) − e_bmatch(t)], with L_i = min(T_on, T_bmatch).
  - e(t) is the mean slot error at the post-step state t, recomputed from the saved poses in
    `results/final_test/steps/`.
  - Pairs with L_i = 0 are excluded and counted under `excluded`; there were none.
  - `estimate` is the equal-actor mean (in the seed and actor rows, the mean over that scope's actors).
  - `boot_lo`/`boot_hi` appear on pooled rows only. They are a 95% stratified paired percentile
    bootstrap within actor with equal actor weights: B = 20,000, `numpy.random.default_rng(20261026)`,
    2.5% and 97.5% quantiles, `method="linear"`. This uses the locked draw order (actors in
    checkpoint-selection order, chunked draws).
  - This is a separate code path. The historical criterion-(c) code was not reused or changed.
  - Cross-check: the recomputed d_i agree with the saved column `d_on_minus_bmatch` in the cells to
    within 5e-8 m, because the saved poses are float32. Eligibility is identical.

## `lost_gained.csv`: exploratory, post hoc

This file covers filter off → filter on, per pair.

- **LOST:** off GOAL and on not GOAL. **GAINED:** off not GOAL and on GOAL.
- **LOST is split by the filter-on outcome:**
  - `filter_induced_stall`, with sub-rows for obstacle, neighbour, and obstacle+neighbour;
  - `other_timeout`, with sub-rows for near-goal parking stall, other stall, and slow progress;
  - `infeasible`;
  - `collision`.
- **TIMEOUT categories** are read directly from `results/final_test/mechanism/timeout_classes.csv`
  (filter-on rows). These categories are exploratory and post hoc.
- **GAINED is split by the filter-off outcome:** `from_collision`, `from_timeout`, `from_infeasible`
  (the last is 0, which is asserted).
- **NET:** `lost_minus_gained` equals `off_goal_minus_on_goal`. This is asserted for every scope. For
  each density, (LOST − GAINED)/n equals the unrounded criterion-(b) estimate in `verdict.json` to
  within 1e-12.
- **`LOST_REACHED_REGION`:** LOST pairs split by the saved filter-on flag `reached_goal_region` (0/1).
  `share` is the share of LOST.
  - The flag means the centroid came within 0.30 m of the goal before any failure.
  - Entering the region does not establish a parking stall.
- **Scopes:** `density` 1, 2, 3 (pooled, seed and actor rows), and `all` (K = 1–3 pooled, pooled and
  seed rows). `share` is the count over all pairs in the scope unless `share_of` = `lost`.
