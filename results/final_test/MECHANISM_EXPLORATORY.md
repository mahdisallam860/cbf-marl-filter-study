# Mechanism analysis of the final test: EXPLORATORY, POST HOC

**EXPLORATORY, POST HOC.** Computed from the saved final-test logs (commits e23f8c3, 41ae3d4); no simulation was run and no confirmatory result changes (the verdict in `FINAL_TEST.md` stands). Categories, thresholds and quantities were chosen after the confirmatory results were seen. Generated by `scripts/mechanism_exploratory.py`; tables in `mechanism/`.

## Plain-language summary (exploratory, post hoc)

* **Timeouts.** With the filter on, 15,038 trials timed out. 9,211 (61%) are filter-induced stalls: the team sits in front of obstacles while the actor keeps commanding motion the barrier rows forbid (obstacle rows involved in 9,143). On the same layouts the filter-off run collided in 5,699, reached the goal in 2,274 and timed out in 1,238. 5,670 (38%) are near-goal parking stalls, 5,362 of them on layouts where the filter-off run stalled the same way: a property of the actor, not of the filter. Very few timeouts are still moving at the horizon. B-match shows the same pattern with more filter-induced stalls (10,557 of 16,051).
* **Infeasible QPs** (433 trials) occur early (median period 36-60), near the entry of the obstacle field (centroid about 2.1-2.7 m from the goal), for a robot that has two neighbour rows and one to three obstacle rows enabled; the nominal command violates an obstacle row in 425 of 433 cases, with the robot about 0.08 m from an obstacle surface and 0.12 m from a neighbour. On 412 of these layouts the filter-off run went on to collide.
* **Equal-split cap.** At alpha = 10 the robot-robot cap on closing speed is rarely binding during transit: its median (about 0.72-0.78 m/s) exceeds the largest barrier-point speed the velocity box allows (0.50 m/s), the closing robot moves at about 0.4 of it (median), and it binds in 1-8% of moving closing periods (filter-off motion exceeds it in 1.5-11.6%). The formation and GOAL losses at alpha* appear to come mainly from obstacle rows, not from trailing robots being held back by the equal split.
* These readings are post hoc and descriptive. They do not change the confirmatory verdict, and they say nothing about other gains, actors or a modified filter.
