A *near-goal timeout* is a TIMEOUT whose final centroid-to-goal distance is <= 0.30 m (the slot radius; obstacle centres are >= 0.75 m from the goal by construction). 'Fails' = which goal criterion is unmet at the final state (centroid within 0.10 m, mean slot error within 0.10 m). Window = last 50 control periods (the protocol's stall window). Lateral / longitudinal = mean over robots of the body-frame components of the error to the absolute target goal + slot_i.
