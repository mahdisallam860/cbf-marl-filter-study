# Journal-Fit Reviewer (EIC) report

**Manuscript:** "An Execution-Time CBF-QP Safety Filter on Frozen MAPPO Formation Policies: A Pre-Specified Paired Evaluation in Simulation"
**Target venues considered:** IEEE RA-L, IEEE L-CSS

## 1. Summary and recommendation

The paper evaluates a decentralised sampled-data CBF-QP filter, added at execution time, on nine frozen MAPPO actors in a VMAS three-robot formation task. The paired design is locked in advance, with three obstacle counts and 9,000 pairs per count. The filter removes collisions and sharply lowers COLLISION-or-INFEASIBLE, but it misses the locked non-inferiority margins for GOAL (5 pp) and common-prefix formation error (0.01 m) at every density. The verdict is therefore "not shown useful", and the post hoc analyses that follow are clearly labelled as exploratory. The confirmatory reporting is unusually careful. Lines 642–643 correctly separate "non-inferiority not established" from "shown inferior", every exploratory section is labelled, and the per-step logs and scripts are released. That care is a real strength.

The interpretive layer has problems. The paper ties the infeasibility to α∆=1, which the audit evidence does not show. It uses causal labels ("filter-induced", "in front of obstacles") that contradict its own disclaimers. It leaves out of the abstract and conclusion that the nominal policies failed the study's own quality gate, that the margins are unjustified, and that at K=1 the miss is a near-miss driven mainly by one seed. Several terms that carry the argument are never defined in the paper, including the stand-off h⋆, the TIMEOUT classes, A1–A9, the layouts, "Stage 2" and "ZR". The contribution is a careful but narrow negative result with no new control-theoretic content. That makes it a poor fit for L-CSS and a possible fit for RA-L only if the paper is cut to the letter length.

**Recommendation: Major revision (RA-L); poor fit for L-CSS.** The confirmatory core is sound and honestly reported, but the abstract, contributions and discussion over-attribute mechanism and under-report scope limits. The manuscript is also about 2.5× the letter length.

### Genuine strengths (brief)
- The confirmatory language is precise: lines 641–643 distinguish "non-inferiority not established" from "shown to exceed the margin", and line 414 says a pooled result does not establish usefulness, or its absence, per seed.
- The design is paired across three arms with reserved layouts, the gain is selected on separate validation data, and the paper includes a brake comparator that many filter papers omit.
- The paper discloses its deviations unusually fully, including the protocol wording correction, the Newcombe label issue, the A5 exception and the seed-1 resume.
- The per-step logs and number-generating scripts make the post hoc analyses repeatable without re-running trials.
