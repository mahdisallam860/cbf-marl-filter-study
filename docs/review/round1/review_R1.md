# Reviewer 1 (Methodology and statistics)

Manuscript: "An Execution-Time CBF-QP Safety Filter on Frozen MAPPO Formation Policies: A Pre-Specified Paired Evaluation in Simulation"

## 1. Summary and recommendation

The paper reports a locked, paired, three-arm evaluation (filter off, filter on at alpha* = 10, B-match) of nine frozen MAPPO actors on 9,000 reserved layouts per density. Usefulness at each density is an intersection-union of (a) an exact one-sided McNemar test on COLLISION-or-INFEASIBLE, (b) GOAL non-inferiority (margin 5 pp) and (c) common-prefix formation non-inferiority (margin 0.01 m). I checked the design against `docs/PREFILTER_VALIDATION_PROTOCOL_LOCKED_2026-10-25.md`, `config/evaluation_protocol_locked.toml`, `results/final_test/{FINAL_TEST.md, verdict.json, paired_timeout_stalled.csv, MECHANISM_EXPLORATORY.md, INFEASIBILITY_AUDIT.md}`, `results/paper_revision/{newcombe_method10_check.csv, time_to_goal.csv}` and `cbf_study/stats.py`. What matches:

- The endpoints, denominators, L_i = 0 exclusion rule, equal-actor estimand, bootstrap settings (B = 20,000, seed 20261026, type-7 quantile), minimum-eligible rule, reached-goal-region definition and gain-selection rule all match the protocol.
- Every Table 3 number matches `verdict.json`.
- The method 8 / method 10 disclosure is accurate. Newcombe (1998) numbers the uncorrected-phi score method as 8 and the phi-corrected one as 10. Every 98.33% bound moves by less than 0.001 pp.

The confirmatory verdict, "not shown useful at K = 1, 2, 3", is correct. The paper is also careful in several places (the H2 formation wording, the density-trend caveat, the joint-success caveat).

The remaining problems are in interpretation and reporting:

1. **(b) at K = 1.** The point estimate (4.81 pp) lies inside the margin, and the paper never says so. It also states that completion "fell" under a non-inferiority criterion.
2. **H3 uses a non-discriminating flag.** The stalled flag fires on essentially every TIMEOUT in both arms, including the unfiltered one.
3. **Seed heterogeneity at K = 1 is not reported.** The formation failure at K = 1 is concentrated in seed 1. Five of nine actors have descriptive intervals below both margins.
4. **The alpha split is not explained.** The paper never says that 0.05/3 is a Bonferroni split over the three density claims, with no split across the IUT components.
5. **Labels are applied inconsistently.** "Pre-declared" and "post hoc" are used inconsistently in several places, and two stall-class definitions are incomplete.

**Recommendation: Minor revision (borderline major).** The design, computations and confirmatory verdict are sound and match the locked protocol. Every required change is a wording or reporting fix that uses results already in the repository, and none requires new trials. The four MAJOR items must be fixed, because as written they invite readers to over-read (b) at K = 1, H3 and the pooled K = 1 verdict.
